Weapons: (Cost: 1000qp)
--------
Level Based: Below level 80, damage is LevelD4.
             Level 80 and above, damage is LevelD5. 
             Hitroll bonus of level/10 (Minimum of 5).
             Damroll bonus of level/10 (Minimum of 5).

Other Info : Weapons can be sold back to questor for 500qp as long as 
             you are the owner. The number in brackets is their default
             weight but note that weight can be changed with trivia points.

             Sword    : Slice - Flaming flag.         (35)
             Axe      : Cleave - Vorpal flag.         (20)
             Staff    : Thwack - Shocking flag.       (15)
             Whip     : Shocking Bite - Vampiric flag.(15)
             Dagger   : Pierce - Sharp Flag.          (10)
             Mace     : Pound - Frost Flag.           (20)
             Flail    : Beating - Vampiric Flag.      (25)
             Halberd  : Drain - Changing Flag.        (25)

             
Shield: (Cost: 750qp)
-------
Base Stats : +5 Strength. Weighs 25.

Level Based: AC is level of player when shield is purchased.
             Hitroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             Damroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).


Aura of Sanctuary: (Cost: 2500qp)
------------------
Base Stats : +200 moves, +50 mana, +100 hp.
             +3 Dexterity. Weighs 1.
             Permanent sanctuary affect when worn.


Amulet of Aardwolf: (Cost: 750qp)
-------------------
Base Stats : +1 Intelligence.
               +1 Dexterity.
               +1 Strength.
               +1 Wisdom.
               +1 Constitution. Weighs 4.

The Amulet is a teleporter to the Aardwolf Hotel with enhanced
healing and a few other nice surprises.


Bracers of Iron Grip: (Cost: 1750qp)
---------------------

Base Stats : A person wearing these bracers cannot be disarmed.
             Weight 5. No AC.

Level Based: Hitroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             Damroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             Hit point bonus of character level (Minimum of 50).
             Mana bonus of character level (Minimum of 50).
These are worn on the arms, NOT the wrist.


Gloves of Dexterity: (Cost: 2250qp)
--------------------

Base stats : A person wearing these gloves has the equivalent of 100%
             dual wield skill regardless of class. 6 points of Dex.     
             Weight 10. No AC.

Level based: Hit point bonus of character level (Minimum of 50).
             Move point bonus of character level (Minimum of 50).
             Mana bonus of character level (Minimum of 50).
             Hitroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             Damroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).


Note: All standard weight restrictions on dual wield still apply and 100%
      dual wield does *NOT* mean 'second attack dual' and 'third attack 
      dual'. These gloves will give a character not already using dual
      wield 1 extra attack per round, every round.

Boots of Speed: (Cost: 1300qp)
---------------
Base Stats : +75hp.
             +10 hitroll, +10 damroll.
             +3 Dexterity.
             Permanent haste affect when worn. Weighs 2.

Level Based: AC is level of player when boots are purchased.


Wings of Aardwolf: (Cost: 800qp)
------------------

Base Stats : +2 Wisdom.
             +2 Constitution.
             +3 Dexterity.
             Permanent flying affect when worn. Weighs 1.


Ring of Invisibility: (Cost: 500qp)
---------------------
Base Stats : +30hp, +30mana, +30moves. Weighs 1.
             Permanent invisibility affect when worn.


Ring of Regeneration: (Cost: 1000qp)
---------------------
Base Stats : +12 hitroll, +12 damroll. Weighs 1.
             Enhanced Healing when worn.


Helm of True Sight: (Cost: 1100qp)
-------------------
Base Stats : +8 hitroll, +8 damroll. Weighs 5.
             +45 hp.
             When worn gives: Detect invis, Detect Hidden, Detect 
                              evil, Detect Good and Detect Magic.

Level Based: AC is level of player when helm is purchased.

Breastplate of Magic Resistance: (Cost: 2000qp)
--------------------------------
Base Stats : +125 moves, +125 mana, +125 hp. Weighs 10.
             Intelligence + 2.
             Constitution + 2.
             Wisdom + 2.
             Dexterity + 2.
             Strength + 2.
             Enhanced Magic Resistance while worn.

Level Based: Hitroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             Damroll bonus of level/5 (Minimum of 5).
             AC is level of player when plate is purchased.


Decanter of Endless Water: (Cost: 550qp)
--------------------------

Bag of Aardwolf: (Cost: 650qp) 
----------------
Base Stats : Holds 1000. Items placed inside count as only 20% of their
             weight.

Level Based: Bag has a negative weight of 50 + ( player level * 2 ).
             So, for example, a level 100 bag weighs -250.
             Holds an additional 20 per level. So, a level 200 bag
             holds 5000.

             As you gain levels, the stats of the bag will increase 
             with you. Buying a higher level bag from someone else 
             serves no purpose as the bag will be set to your own
             level.

IMPORTANT
---------

Please read 'HELP REIMBURSEMENT' for details in what happens should you
lose a quest item and the circumstances in which they can and cannot be
reimbursed.
